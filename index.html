      Reading the chart

      A bar spans the full term its course sits in, so an overlap means two commitments share teaching weeks, not necessarily the same slot. Stacked bars in one lane are simultaneous; the red wash marks exactly which weeks are doubled up.

      DPM 19 and DPM 20 are both second-year cohorts, but one runs on terms and the other on semesters. Those two calendars drifting against each other, and against the practice and teaching tracks, is where most of the doubling up comes from.
